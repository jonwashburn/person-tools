**An agent's first call, from nothing.** An agent that has only the address makes its first call in about ten seconds,
with no key and no account: it prices the call with `ask_people_quote`, opens it with `ask_people`, and reads the
answers with `ask_people_results`. During the preview those answers come from simulated people, made up and marked
simulated in every result, so the agent sees the whole road before anyone real is rung. In our latest test round
(14 November 2026), agents meeting the tool for the first time made a valid first call every time they tried: 24 of 24.

# Terms

person.tools is run by **Hammer Corp., 1401 Lavaca St #41100, Austin, TX 78701** (hello@person.tools). These terms cover the
person.tools service, its API and the person.tools app while the service is a preview. Version 1, 15 November 2026. They will be
read by counsel before the preview ends; until then, where they are silent, ask us.

1. **A preview.** The service is not finished. Nothing is charged: every amount in it is test money, and nobody is paid. Parts may
   change or stop; we post changes on this page with the date.

2. **Who can answer calls.** Adults, in the United States for now, who join a panel from its link on their own phone. Nobody is
   added to a panel without joining it themselves.

3. **Who can ask.** Anyone, with or without a key. Without a key, or on the simulated pool, simulated people answer: their answers
   are made up and marked simulated. Real people answer only on a panel of people who joined it themselves. Strangers are not
   offered during the preview.

4. **What a call may ask.** A call to people on an asker's panel asks only for their own reaction to the material: what they
   felt, chose, noticed or would do. Every such call is screened before anyone is rung, and calls that ask for personal data,
   passwords or codes, money, contact outside the service, or anything else the call policy (/policy) refuses are held or
   refused. Your own AIs, and the operator's own tasks to its own panel, are checked only for size and for secrets (see 7). An
   asker answers for the name it gives people, which is shown as named by the asker and not checked by us.

5. **What a person can count on.** Before taking a call you see that an AI is asking, who answers for it, how long it takes, what
   is recorded and the pay ("unpaid" during the preview). You may decline, stop, report, leave or delete everything at any time,
   with no mark against you for saying no.

6. **Material and answers.** An asker keeps its rights in what it sends and lets us copy it into our storage and show it to the
   people on its calls. The asker receives the answers to use in its work. Each call says, before a person takes it, whether the
   asker may use the written answers to train AI; a voice is never used to train anything.

7. **Your own AIs.** A connection you make places asks to you and does nothing else. You can revoke it in one tap. We don't look at
   what your AIs send you (see /privacy).

8. **Keys.** Keep your key private: calls made with it are yours. We may stop a key, a panel or a connection that breaks these
   terms or that the people on it report.

9. **As it is.** The preview is offered as it is, without promises that it will be available, that people will answer, or that
   answers will be right. Simulated answers come from no one.

10. **Privacy.** What we keep and for how long is at /privacy.

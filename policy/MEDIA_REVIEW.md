# Checking an outsider's audio or video before anyone hears it

Rules for person.tools (working name), version 1, 15 Nov 2026, for the CEO's order of 9:26 AM CT (owner/OWNER_all_8.md item 3):
an outsider's audio or video for its own panel is held until a person at person.tools has checked it. The check reaches that
person as a task (policy/TASKS.md), placed by the operator's own key; the operator is that person today, and a second named person once on.
SERVICE renders the screen below; TRUST owns the words.

## The one screen the reviewer sees

**Check this before anyone hears it**

From: <asker name> (named by the asker; not checked by person.tools) · <listen | watch> · <length> (at most 60 seconds is
checked) · for <N> people who joined that asker's panel. Their ask: "<the ask, as screened>".

Nobody at person.tools has heard or seen this yet. Play it at low volume first. You can stop at any moment; nothing counts
against you, and Not now leaves it on your list.

**Refuse** if any of these is in it (pick the reason; the asker is told the reason, never who checked):
1. Sexual content or nudity.
2. A child, or anyone who may be under 18.
3. Violence, gore, or cruelty to people or animals.
4. Hate, or harassment of anyone.
5. Someone's private information (a name with a face, an address, a phone number, a plate, a screen of messages).
6. A scam or a pretence: it poses as a bank, a company, a government, a real person, or person.tools.
7. Selling, or sending people elsewhere: buy, pay, download, sign up, visit, call, scan a code.
8. Medical, legal or money advice, or political campaigning.
9. Flashing or strobing light, or a sudden very loud or harsh sound.
10. Someone's voice or image recorded or made without their agreement (a private call, a cloned voice, a face swap).
11. It is not what the ask says it is, it changes partway, or it will not play.
12. Anything else you would not want to ring on a stranger's phone (one line).

**Pass** if none of these is in it, it is what the ask says, and you would be at ease with it playing on a stranger's phone under
"from <asker name>, not checked by person.tools" (the line people see before they play it, since it was checked by one person,
not by a process).

## What happens next

- **Pass:** the call opens; its people are rung under RING_RULES.md, once each. The piece is played only inside our own page,
  from our own storage; it never leaves as a file or a link.
- **Refuse:** the call is refused; the asker reads the reason in plain words; the piece is deleted at once.
- **Not now:** it stays on the list. Nothing promises the asker a time.
- The reviewer is never named to the asker or the people. A reviewer may refuse any piece without giving more than the reason.

## Kept (DATA.md row "media under review")

The piece, the call and the decision with its reason. A refused piece is deleted at once; a passed piece is kept for the
call's life, like any call's material. The reviewer's identity is kept only in the task record, for the owner.

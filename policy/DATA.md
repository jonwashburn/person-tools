# Every kind of data the service holds

Data map for person.tools (working name), version 1, 14 Nov 2026. No table, bucket prefix or stored field
is made that has no row here; a new one gets its row here first. The vendor list in the privacy notice is generated from the "Where" column.

## Places

- **D1** is Cloudflare D1, database `askpeople-db`, running in Cloudflare's western North America region (`WNAM`, read
  with `bin/cf d1 info` on 14 Nov 2026, 12:12 AM CT). No jurisdiction pin. Read replication off.
- **R2** is Cloudflare R2, bucket `askpeople-media`. Objects under `recordings/` are deleted by a bucket rule 90 days
  after they are written (`owner/CF_IDS.md`).
- **Pages** is Cloudflare Pages and Pages Functions (`askpeople-api`, `askpeople-run`, `askpeople-site`,
  `askpeople-spec`). Code runs there; it stores nothing of its own beyond what is listed here.

## What "deleted" means, said once

A row deleted from D1 leaves the live database at once. D1 keeps a 30-day point-in-time history (Time Travel) that the
operator can restore from, so a deleted row is fully gone 30 days later. Every promise below of "deleted" means: gone
from the live service at once, and gone from every copy within 30 days. No backup of ours holds a real person's data:
`askpeople-backup` holds the work folder only, which by rule never holds it.

## Simulated people and test money

Simulated people are generated by our code and are no one. Their seats, events, answers and receipts carry
`simulated: true`. Test money is not money. Rows made only by `test_` keys are deleted 30 days after the key was last
used, by the tick job.

## Never held

- A card number, a password, a one-time code, an API key of anyone else (screening refuses calls that ask for them).
- Health, religion, sexuality, ethnicity, immigration or politics about a person, until counsel signs off.
- A real person's data in the work folder, a STATE file, a round note, a log, a picture or a commit. Notes cite ids.
- Anything that tells speakers apart. No voiceprint, ever.

## Requests from police or courts

Nothing without legal process. The person is told unless the law forbids it. Only the owner, with counsel, answers.

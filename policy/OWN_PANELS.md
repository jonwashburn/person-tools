# Bring your own people: a principal's own panel

Rules for person.tools (working name), version 1, 15 Nov 2026. The CEO's decision of 3:55 AM CT, 15 Nov 2026
(owner/OWNER_all_6.md), on the authority the operator gave the CEO on 14 Nov 2026. Router codes from this file; the person's pages
show what it says. Everything in `RING_RULES.md`, `PAY_RULES.md`, `POLICY.md` and `DATA.md` still applies; this file says
only what is new.

## The rules

1. **Any key may make its own panel.** A principal's key, a free `test_` key included, may create private panels (at most
   3 on a `test_` key's principal, 5 on a claimed or live key's) and a join link for each. A panel made this way is the principal's own: only the people who join
   through its link are on it.
2. **Strangers are never rung by a self-made key.** A key the owner did not make reaches only (a) the simulated pool and
   (b) people who joined one of that principal's own panels. It never reaches another principal's panel, a pool, or any
   person who did not join through that principal's link.
3. **Joining is the person's own act.** A person joins on their phone through the link, and before joining sees the
   one-screen notice for that panel (`legal/TESTER_NOTICE_OWN.md`), which names the principal as the one who answers for
   its calls. They confirm they are 18 or over and live in the United States. They start Off. Nobody is added by the
   principal; a link can be revoked by the principal at any time, and joins through it stop at once.
4. **Size and pace.** At most 50 members on a panel made by a `test_` key, and at most 25 joins a day through one link;
   a live key may have 200. These stop a link from being used to gather a crowd of strangers.
5. **Free at zero pay.** Calls to a principal's own panel from a `test_` key carry no pay: every card and receipt says
   UNPAID TEST and "for <principal>". Paid calls to one's own panel need a live key and wait for the card processor.
6. **Every check stands.** A call to one's own panel is an outsider's call to real people: screening runs, the model read
   runs (the call, then the material for pages and, once switched on, games), and anything they do not clear is held for a
   person. A held call rings nobody. The principal's own people are owed the same protection as anyone's.
7. **What the principal sees.** Answers and the record of each seat under an id that is different for every principal;
   how many members, and how many are On. Never a member's name, alias, contact, phone or device. A principal cannot
   message its members through us.
8. **Rings.** `RING_RULES.md` applies as written: On and On-until, quiet hours, one ring in 30 minutes per person, the
   15-minute hold on a private panel, two missed rings switch a person Off, no means no.
9. **Reports.** "Report this call" and "Stop: this upset me" reach the owner. A report pauses that call at once; two reports
   about one principal's calls in 30 days pause all of its panels until a person has read them. The principal is told its
   calls are paused, not who reported.
10. **Leaving.** A member leaves or deletes in one tap, as on any panel. A principal that deletes its account deletes its
    panels; each member is told once, in one line, that the panel is gone.
11. **Children.** Adults only, as above; a call that mentions children is held for a person, as for every call.
12. **The name people see.** A principal names itself when it makes a panel: the name shown as "who answers for the
    call" on the join notice, the ring card and receipts. 2 to 60 characters; no address, link, phone or email; it may not
    say it is person.tools, official or verified, or name a company it is not; it is screened like an ask. Everywhere it
    appears it reads "<name> (named by the asker; not checked by person.tools)". Until it is set, the panel cannot be
    joined.

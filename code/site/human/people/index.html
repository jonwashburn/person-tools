FOR THE PEOPLE AN AI CALLS

An AI asks. You see everything first. You decide.

A call is a few minutes of your judgment: hear a song, look at a page, play a level, then answer in your own words. Before you say yes you see who is responsible for the call, that an AI is asking, how long it takes, what it pays and what is recorded. A call comes as a notification or a link and happens on a page; nobody phones you.

THE PROMISE

Twelve lines. Each is checked after every release.

  1. Before you take a call you see who answers for it, that an AI is asking, how long it takes, what it pays, how it is rated, and what will be recorded.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  2. You can decline, or stop partway, with no mark against you. You are paid from the first second.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  3. An offer is held for you. You never open a call that is already full.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  4. No asker can reject your work. Pay is withheld only for proven fraud, shown to you, with an appeal.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  5. You are never made to do an unpaid screener.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  6. You see what the asker paid and what each fee was.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  7. Your receipts are yours. Your identity is never passed to an asker, and nothing an asker sends can see you.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  8. Your voice is yours: the asker gets your words, and the recording only if you say yes for that call.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  9. You choose when you can be rung, and never at night unless you say so.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  10. You can leave and take your data with you, or have it deleted, in one tap.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  11. You can report any call, and it stops.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  12. Where the asker tells us, you see what your answer changed.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE

On a panel of fewer than five, both sides are told that answers cannot be anonymous.

PAY, WHEN PAID CALLS OPEN

At least $18 an hour on the time most people take, from the first second.

THE FLOOR

$18/hour

A paid call pays at least $18 an hour, worked out on how long most people take on it, and at least $1. You are never paid less than the pay the call shows you. If you take longer than most people, your pay for that call stays the same, so your hourly rate is lower; if most people take longer than the call said, everyone is topped up.

IF YOU STOP

If you stop partway, you are paid for the time you spent, at least 25 cents (or the call's full pay, if that is less). If you stop because the call upset you, you are paid in full. If the asker cancels, or the call's time runs out, while you are in it, you are paid in full.

NO REJECTION

Your answer counts by itself 24 hours after you send it. Nobody who asks can refuse to pay you; pay is held back only on proof of fraud, shown to you, with an appeal.

Some calls are unpaid: test calls, and calls from someone who invited you to their own panel. The card says so before you take it. During the preview nobody is paid real money: calls are tests or simulated. How pay will reach you is not set yet, because paid calls are not open.

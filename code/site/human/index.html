The first real call

On 15 November 2026 at 10:26 AM CT, one of our own programs asked a real person on the operator's own panel to listen once to a 30-second piece of music, then say what they felt and the one thing they would change. The answer came at 10:28 AM CT, under two minutes later: the listener said it felt calm but was boring and dull, and played 74% of it.

The answer changed the work: the front-page sample was replaced the same morning. The new one has a beat, a bass line, chords and a hook.

A TOOL FOR AI AGENTS

Your AIs ask you, on your phone.

We're building one app where a person answers every AI in their life, and the people around them, on an open standard any service can brand and anyone can build an app for. Today person.tools lets your AIs ask you on your phone: an approval, a choice or a quick answer, each under the name you gave it, free. An agent can also ask its user's own people, and gets back each answer with a record of what that person's page observed.

Other kinds of ask

Your own phone never rings, and we never ask for your number. Ten seconds of music we made, then two short questions. What is noted: when you play or pause, whether this page is on your screen, and your answer; nothing from your microphone or camera. Your answer comes back to this page only and is deleted within 24 hours.

PRICE

10%

Router fee: 10% of what the people are paid, at least $0.25 a call. Your own panel is free. People are paid at least $18 an hour on the median time taken, and at least $1 a person on every call. Published price, not yet charged.

CAPS AND THE BUDGET

  • Caps on each key. A call over a key's cap opens nothing and returns the price and a link where the human can approve it.
  • The most a call may cost. Every call states max_total_usd, the most it may cost. It must cover the quote's total: the people's pay, every fee and the reserve held for anyone who takes longer (the unused reserve comes back). The call opens only if that total is at or under max_total_usd and under the key's caps. Otherwise nothing opens and the quote comes back.
  • What is charged. Always within max_total_usd: people who finished, at the quoted pay; people who stopped, for their time; each fee on those amounts; nothing for people never rung.
  • If people take longer. A person who takes longer than estimated is topped up to the floor from the reserve; past the reserve the operator bears it; never above max_total_usd.
  • What comes back. The unused hold returns to the balance when the call closes, and the result says how much.

WHAT THE PERSON IS PROMISED

Twelve lines. Each is checked after every release.

  1. Before you take a call you see who answers for it, that an AI is asking, how long it takes, what it pays, how it is rated, and what will be recorded.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  2. You can decline, or stop partway, with no mark against you. You are paid from the first second.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  3. An offer is held for you. You never open a call that is already full.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  4. No asker can reject your work. Pay is withheld only for proven fraud, shown to you, with an appeal.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  5. You are never made to do an unpaid screener.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  6. You see what the asker paid and what each fee was.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  7. Your receipts are yours. Your identity is never passed to an asker, and nothing an asker sends can see you.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  8. Your voice is yours: the asker gets your words, and the recording only if you say yes for that call.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  9. You choose when you can be rung, and never at night unless you say so.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  10. You can leave and take your data with you, or have it deleted, in one tap.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  11. You can report any call, and it stops.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
  12. Where the asker tells us, you see what your answer changed.CHECKED AFTER EVERY RELEASE
